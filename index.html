Who We Are

Two industries, one register — planted the same day the company was born.

David & Rain Nig Ltd was incorporated on 1 June 2026 under the Companies and Allied Matters Act, 2020, as a private company limited by shares, registered at 22 Dimrose, Yenegoa, Bayelsa State. The company was founded to run agriculture and construction side by side, treating the two as a single supply chain rather than separate businesses.

On the agricultural side, the company cultivates crops, raises livestock, runs fisheries, and processes and distributes the resulting produce. On the construction side, it builds — buildings, roads, drainage, and the site infrastructure that agricultural land needs before anything can grow on it. The same crews that clear and prepare land for planting are equipped to lay foundations and roads on it.

Land first

Site preparation and land development sit at the hinge between our two sectors — the starting point for both a farm and a foundation.

Family-run

Ownership and direction sit with the Alamieyeseigha Godknows Salo family, based in Port Harcourt and Yenegoa.
